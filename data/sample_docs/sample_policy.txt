SECURELIFE HEALTH INSURANCE — SAMPLE POLICY

What is covered: In-patient hospitalization exceeding 24 hours, including
room rent, ICU charges, doctor's fees, and medicines. Pre-hospitalization
expenses up to 30 days before admission and post-hospitalization up to 60
days after discharge are also covered. Day-care procedures that do not
require a 24-hour hospital stay, such as cataract surgery, dialysis, and
chemotherapy, are covered in full without the 24-hour rule applying.
Ambulance charges are covered up to Rs. 3,000 per hospitalization.

Waiting periods: There is an initial waiting period of 30 days from the
policy start date, during which no claims are payable except those arising
from an accident. Pre-existing diseases are covered only after 36 months
of continuous policy renewal. Specific conditions such as cataracts,
hernia, and joint replacement surgery have a waiting period of 24 months
unless caused by an accident.

What is not covered: Cosmetic or plastic surgery unless required due to
an accident, dental treatment unless requiring hospitalization due to an
accident, treatment for infertility or maternity in the first 9 months of
the policy, self-inflicted injuries, and treatment taken outside India.
Claims arising from adventure sports such as skydiving or scuba diving
are excluded unless an optional adventure sports rider has been purchased.

Claims process: For cashless claims, the policyholder must get treatment
at a network hospital and inform SecureLife at least 48 hours before a
planned admission, or within 24 hours for emergency admissions. For
reimbursement claims at non-network hospitals, the policyholder must
submit the claim form along with original bills, discharge summary, and
diagnostic reports within 30 days of discharge. Reimbursement is
typically processed within 15 working days of receiving a complete claim
file.

Sum insured and renewal: The policy offers sum insured options of Rs. 3
lakh, 5 lakh, 10 lakh, and 25 lakh. A cumulative bonus of 10% of the sum
insured is added for every claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50% of the
base sum insured. The policy is renewable for life, subject to timely
premium payment.

Co-payment and room rent limits: A co-payment of 20% applies to all
claims if the policyholder is above 60 years of age at the time of
hospitalization. Room rent is capped at 1% of the sum insured per day for
a normal room, and 2% of the sum insured per day for ICU; any expense
above this cap is borne proportionally by the policyholder.
